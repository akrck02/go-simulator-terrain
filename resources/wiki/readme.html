🚧 Not done yet

  • The erosion rule only cuts and rounds: no sediment is laid down (no deltas, alluvial fans or filled valleys), uplift is the same everywhere, and every rock gives way alike (one erodibility for the whole map). Without it, the relief is read and published once.
  • No aspect (which way a slope faces) and no catchments; the flow itself only shows as drainage_area and the rivers.
  • .npy reliefs must be two-dimensional little-endian float32 or float64 arrays in C order; Fortran-ordered arrays are refused.
  • The cells are square: a relief with different spacings east and north cannot be read.
  • examples/ground.yaml does not come with a relief: put your own relieve.json and relieve.f32 next to it (see getting started).
  • go-simulator’s 3D viewer draws the scene’s own ground, not these fields, so there are no screenshots of the viewer here; the pictures are 2D drawings of the published fields (see how the pictures were made).
